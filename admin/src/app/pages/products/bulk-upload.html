1. Download the Excel template

The Instructions sheet explains every field, how to list multiple colors, and how to map shared or color-specific images.

2. Fill in product and color rows

Add one row per color (three colors means three rows). Keep the same SKU and product details on every row; change color_name, color_hex and color_images for each color. List multiple images with semicolons. Use existing category slugs.
