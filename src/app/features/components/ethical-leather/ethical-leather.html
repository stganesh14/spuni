OUR LEATHER PROMISE

No animal is harmed for our leather.

Every SPUNI hide is a by-product of the food industry: skins that would otherwise be thrown away. We rescue them, tan them responsibly and craft them into pieces made to last for decades. Nothing is killed for beauty. Nothing is wasted.
